# DIG105 — Namish Malneedi

## My Digital Product Journey Starts Here

This repository is my workspace for **DIG105 Digital Product Development and Prototyping** at Estonian Business School.

I am approaching the course as a **designer**: start from a real user need, shape a clear product vision, then turn that vision into a prototype people can try.

### Why this course

A digital product succeeds when it is tested with users and revised from what they actually do — not from assumptions. This space is where I will move from idea to a functional, validated prototype using design thinking, visual design, and modern AI / low-code / vibe-coding tools.

### How I will work

1. **Define the problem** — who it is for, what hurts, and what success looks like
2. **Set functional requirements** — the smallest set of capabilities that prove the idea
3. **Design visually** — flows, screens, and interaction before committing to implementation
4. **Prototype** — turn the design into something clickable and usable
5. **Validate** — test, listen, and iterate before Demo Day
